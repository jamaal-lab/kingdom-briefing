Welcome to Greater Harvest Church
As a changed people, changing our community, the Greater Harvest Church remains steadfast in its efforts to lead God’s people to a place of wholeness in Jesus Christ.  Following the example of Christ, we offer hope, healing, restoration, salvation and liberation in a contemporary context. 
At Greater Harvest, we pursue excellence in service, consecration in worship and fortitude in faith. As a body of believers we support the edification of our minds through teaching and charitable deeds through Christian service.
We hold dear the mandate of Jesus Christ to love God, love people and impact the world.  Our sincere hope is that when you enter you will be able to say, “I am glad to be here!” Upon leaving, know that a warm welcome awaits your return.
In the meantime, Let us not grow weary in doing what is right, for we will reap at harvest time, if we do not give up.    Galatians 6:9 NRSV
Rev. Sebrone O. Johnson
