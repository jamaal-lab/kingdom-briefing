 Diaconate Ministry 
Diaconate Ministry:
Together with the Pastor, the Diaconate Ministry exercises chief responsibility for the services of worship. They provide pulpit supplies. They prepare and assist at the observance of the Ordinance of the Lord’s Supper and the Ordinance of Believer’s Baptism. They assist the Pastor in visitation and counseling.
The Deacons keep a watchful eye over the needy, the sick, and the afflicted in the church and the community, and are ready to render aid.  They support the Christian growth of both the congregation and of individual members.  They are here to serve you.

 Trustee Ministry 
The Trustee Ministry strives through the power of the Holy Spirit, to be used as a vessel for Christ in this branch of Zion. With Christ as our example, and His ministry as our model, we serve in unity, we embrace the vision of the church and support the work of the Pastor in the perfecting of the saints for the work of the ministry for the edifying of the body of Christ.
We support the ministry of the Church by giving oversight to maintenance and care of church properties; managing the fiscal operations; serving as legal custodians; and promoting the unity of the body of Christ at Greater Harvest Church.  We are always looking for volunteers to help with our Building and Grounds Ministry and Security Ministry.

The Greeters Ministry
To offers new visitors and established church members alike a chance to feel loved, cared for and respected. It is designed to make worshipers feel comfortable and at ease in our church.

Members of our Greeters Ministry will probably be, for most, the first people our guests will encounter in the process of finding their way into the community of Greater Harvest Church. This is both a great challenge and a great responsibility.  We would love you to be a part.

 Missionary Ministry 
The Greater Harvest Church Missionary Ministry is committed to fulfilling the greater mission of the faith community: to unite the faith community in a fuller understanding and participation in the responsibility for worldwide witness and ministry; to work to cultivate a missionary conviction into the hearts of all people; to provide resources for in-depth mission and evangelism education.
We frequently visit the sick, sponsor clothing and food drives.  We keep healthy connections with all members to promote internal and external evangelism efforts.  We would love for you to take an active role in the ministry.
