Good questions, straight answers.

Still deciding? Start here.

Do I need a whole new website?

Not always. Many sites need focused upgrades: fixing pages, links and forms, refreshing content and photos, and improving mobile, speed and search. Your score above points to the path that fits.

How is pricing decided?

Every project starts with a written scope. Price and timing are agreed before any work begins, so there are no surprises.

Who will I work with?

Your project details are reviewed personally by Jamaal T. Peavy, Founder and Lead Integrator. You will get a reply to set up a short call.

Can my website connect to booking, email or a CRM?

Yes. Buildouts can include intake, booking and CRM connections, so confirmations and follow up happen without manual copying.

Will you need my passwords?

No. We never ask for passwords or login details in the project form.
