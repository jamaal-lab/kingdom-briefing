Get Involved 
Ambassadors 
The Ambassadors Ministry is the welcoming arm of the church body. 
Bereavement Ministry 
The Bereavement Ministry assists members in making arrangements for funerals held at Fellowship as well provides resolutions on behalf of Fellowship for members, or loved ones of members who have passed away. 
Community Table 
The Community Table serves hot food to the people in the community each Thursday from 12pm-2pm. 
Congregational Care Ministry 
The Congregational Care Ministry serves the sick and immobile Fellowship members by visiting them regularly. The Congregational Care Ministry provides prayer and communicates the needs of the sick and immobile members to the ministers, deacons, and other ministry leaders. 
Credit Union 
The Credit Union is a not-for-profit financial institution. We are a member-owned financial cooperative. We are controlled by the members. We operate by the principle – people helping people. Hours of Operation: Saturday 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM 
Culinary Ministry 
The Culinary Ministry strives to glorify God by providing excellence in food service. This ministry is responsible for feeding God’s people for various programs and oversees the operation of the kitchen, including its use and cleanliness. 
Fellowship Bible Academy 
Fellowship Bible Academy (FBA) is Fellowship’s Christian education course. FBA courses occur in terms each year (Fall, Spring, and/or Summer). Members are able to pick from multiple courses offered to learn more about the Bible and our faith and life application. 
First Touch 
The First Touch Ministry serves as our intake team to greet and welcome new members to our congregation. 
Freely Give 
Freely Give is Fellowship’s food and clothing giveaway which occurs Wednesdays from 7 AM -10 AM and every 4th Saturday of the month from 8 AM to 11 AM 
Girl Scouts 
Fellowship Girl Scouts (Ages 5-18) builds girls of courage, confidence, and character, who make the world a better place. Girl Scouts calendar year begins in September and ends in June. Meetings take place the 2nd and 4th Saturday of each month. 
Get Connected 
Intercessory Prayer Team 
The Intercessory Prayer Team stands in the gap to pray for members, the church, and our leaders. 
Kidz Zone Children’s Ministry (ages 3-11) 
The Children’s Ministry provides a setting for our children to worship, receive instruction, have fellowship, and learn how to be followers of Christ. Children must be potty-trained to attend. 
Kings and Priest Men’s Ministry 
This ministry is designed to enlighten, encourage and empower men to confidently function in their God-ordained role. Through teaching, fellowship, interaction, transparency and accountability, this ministry will build kingdom men of purpose, promise, and power. 
Join A Ministry 
Media Ministry 
The Media Ministry is responsible for all telecommunication (radio, television & internet). 
Media Sales 
The Media Sales team is responsible for product sales. 
Medical Ministry 
The Medical Ministry provides healthcare empowerment through educational resources that positively impact the physical, social, emotional, and psychological well-being of our members and community at large.  Licensed and qualified team members provide aid and assistance when needed. 
Movements of Praise Dance Ministry 
The purpose of this ministry is to surrender our bodies as instruments of praise and worship unto the Lord through dance (Romans 12:1), create an atmosphere of worship, and to enhance each member’s spiritual development and dance technique. 
Music Ministry 
The purpose of this ministry is to minister and intercede for His people through music. This ministry includes the Band, Praise Team, Sanctuary Choir, Men’s Choir, Voices of Genesis Young Adult Choir, & Women’s Choir. 
Peacekeepers Security Ministry 
Our Peacekeepers Ministry is responsible for securing the building during worship services and events. 
Recovery Ministry 
This ministry is designed to address the addictions of the human personality, such as chemical dependency, alcoholism and assist the addicted personality in obtaining sobriety and a sense of well-being by building faith and breaking the chains of addiction through salvation. 
Senior Care Ministry 
Senior Care Ministry serves the seniors of Fellowship by providing important resources and opportunities for socialization. The Senior Care Ministry ensures that the seniors of Fellowship are cared for in and outside of the church. 
Sisters With Vision (SWV) Women’s Ministry 
This ministry is designed to enlighten, encourage and empower women to confidently walk in their royal status. Through teaching, fellowship, interaction, transparency, and accountability, this ministry will build kingdom women of purpose, promise, and power. 
Social Justice Ministry 
The Social Justice Ministry of Fellowship Chicago is committed to creating meaningful change through advocacy, equity efforts, and initiatives that promote systemic justice. This work is accomplished through education, team building, and activism based on the teachings of Christ. 
Soulmates Marriage Ministry 
Our Marriage Ministry empowers marriages to thrive by providing couples with tools and examples for successful marriages, by sharing and showing the gospel of Jesus Christ through acts of love and generosity. 
The Crew Young Adult Ministry 
The Crew Young Adults Ministry’s vision is to edify, empower, encourage, and engage millennials (21-39 years old) through events and experiences, providing them with spiritual tools to assist in living life with Christ! 
V.I.B.E. (Volunteer to Impact, Build & Empower) 
V.I.B.E. volunteer program is designed to develop and utilize genuine people committed to transforming good intentions into great actions with diverse volunteer involvement. 
Violence Support Ministry 
The Violence Support Ministry provides support to grieving family members who have lost loved ones to violent crimes. 
Wave Youth Ministry (Middle School & High School | Ages 12-18) 
Wave Youth Ministry strives to reach middle school & high school students to engage and stimulate them in a way that will help them live out their Christian faith. 
Youth Student Ministries (Middle School & High School | Ages 12-18) 
The Youth Student Ministry strives to reach middle school & high school students to engage and stimulate them in a way that will help them live out their Christian faith.
